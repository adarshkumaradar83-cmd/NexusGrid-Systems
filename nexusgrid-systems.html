System Overview

NexusGrid is a next-generation, 100% client-side workspace for running and monitoring multiple websites side by side — add any number of URLs, arrange them in a responsive node grid, and control refresh, viewport, and simulated network conditions per node or across the whole workspace. Everything persists locally in your browser.
